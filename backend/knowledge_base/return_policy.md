# AETHER INDIA — Return & Refund Policy
**Document Version:** 3.2 | **Effective Date:** 1 October 2024 | **Jurisdiction:** India

---

## 1. Overview

AETHER INDIA is committed to a transparent, customer-first return experience. All return, refund, and exchange requests are initially handled by our AI-powered support system. High-value refund cases are escalated to our human operations team within 24 hours.

---

## 2. Return Eligibility Window

| Condition | Return Window |
|---|---|
| All full-price items | **14 calendar days** from confirmed delivery date |
| Sale / discounted items | **7 calendar days** from confirmed delivery date |
| Limited-edition / collab drops | **Non-returnable** (exchange only within 7 days) |
| Customised or monogrammed items | **Non-returnable, non-exchangeable** |

> Items must be returned in **original, unworn condition** with all tags attached and in original packaging.

---

## 3. Refund Tiers & Processing Rules

AETHER INDIA operates a **dual-tier refund system** designed to balance speed and financial control:

### Tier 1 — Automated AI Refund (≤ ₹5,000)

- Refund amount is **₹5,000 or below**.
- Order status is **Delivered**.
- Item is within the **14-day return window**.
- No quality dispute or policy exception is claimed.
- **Result:** The AI system (`RefundAndPaymentAgent`) automatically approves the refund, updates order status to `Refunded`, initiates the credit, and sends the customer an in-app notification.
- **Timeline:** Credit appears within **3–5 business days** for UPI/Card payments. For COD (Cash on Delivery) orders, a bank transfer is initiated within **5–7 business days**.

### Tier 2 — Human Admin Review (> ₹5,000)

- Refund amount **exceeds ₹5,000**.
- The AI system (`NotificationAndEscalationAgent`) creates a `PENDING_ADMIN` approval ticket in the Admin Dashboard.
- The customer is notified immediately: *"Your refund request of ₹X,XXX has been logged and is under review by our operations team. You will receive a decision within 24–48 hours."*
- **Admin SLA:** Operations team must respond within **48 hours**.
- **Result on Approval:** Order status → `Refunded`; payment credit initiated.
- **Result on Rejection:** Order status remains `Delivered`; AI notifies customer with the admin's reason.

---

## 4. Refund Payment Timelines by Method

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline |
|---|---|
| UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) | 3–5 business days to source UPI ID |
| Credit / Debit Card | 5–7 business days to issuing bank |
| Cash on Delivery (COD) | 5–7 business days via NEFT bank transfer |
| AETHER Store Credit | Instant (credited to account wallet) |

---

## 5. Non-Refundable Situations

The following situations are **not eligible** for a refund:

1. Item returned after the 14-day eligibility window.
2. Item shows signs of wear, washing, alteration, or damage by the customer.
3. Original tags removed or packaging destroyed.
4. Customised, monogrammed, or limited-edition items (as listed in Section 2).
5. Free gifts or promotional items received alongside an order.
6. Shipping and handling charges (non-refundable unless the return is due to our error).

---

## 6. How to Initiate a Return

Customers can initiate a return via **three channels**:

1. **AI Chat (Recommended):** Type *"I want to return my order AETH-XXXX"* in the chat. The `RefundAndPaymentAgent` will verify eligibility, generate an RMA label, and guide you through the process.
2. **My Orders Page:** Navigate to `/orders`, select the order, and click **"Return / Exchange"**.
3. **Email:** Write to `returns@aether.in` with your order number, reason, and photos of the item.

---

## 7. Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA) Labels

- Upon approval, the system generates an **RMA label** (format: `RMA-XXXX`).
- The RMA label is valid for **7 days** from the date of issue.
- The label is pre-paid for returns due to AETHER's fault (wrong item, defective product).
- For customer-initiated returns (change of mind, size issues), a **₹99 reverse pickup fee** is deducted from the refund amount.
- Approved exchanges are **free of reverse pickup charges**.

---

## 8. Size Exchange Policy

- Size exchanges are permitted once per order within the **14-day window**.
- The AI system checks live stock availability for the target size before approving.
- If the target size is out of stock, the customer is offered a full refund (subject to the tier rules above).
- Exchange orders ship within **2–3 business days** of receiving the returned item.

---

## 9. Defective or Wrong Item Policy

If you received a **wrong item** or a **defective/damaged product**:

- Full refund or free re-shipment — your choice.
- No reverse pickup fee charged.
- Refund processed at Tier 1 speed regardless of amount.
- Submit a photo/video of the defect or wrong item via chat or email.

---

## 10. Policy Disputes & Escalation

If you disagree with an AI decision or believe an exception should be granted:

- Type *"I want to escalate this to a human"* or *"Policy dispute for order AETH-XXXX"* in the chat.
- The `NotificationAndEscalationAgent` will create a `POLICY_EXCEPTION` approval ticket in the Admin Dashboard.
- A human operations agent will review and respond within **48 hours**.
