# AETHER INDIA — Size & Fit Guide
**Document Version:** 2.1 | **Effective Date:** 1 March 2024

---

## 1. Introduction

AETHER INDIA designs streetwear in two primary silhouettes: **Boxy** and **Oversized**. Our sizing is inspired by Japanese and Korean streetwear proportions. This guide provides precise measurements to help customers choose the right size before purchasing.

> **Tip:** If you are between two sizes, we recommend **sizing up** for our Boxy fit and **staying true to size** for our Oversized fit, as Oversized garments already have generous ease built in.

---

## 2. How to Measure Yourself

Use a soft measuring tape (in centimetres or inches):

- **Chest:** Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal and snug but not tight.
- **Shoulders:** Measure from the outer edge of one shoulder to the outer edge of the other, across the back.
- **Body Length:** Measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the hem.
- **Sleeve Length:** Measure from the centre back of the neck, across the shoulder, and down to the wrist.
- **Waist:** Measure around the natural waist (narrowest point), keeping one finger between tape and body.
- **Hip:** Measure around the fullest part of the hips.
- **Inseam:** Measure from the crotch seam to the bottom of the leg.

## 7. Fit Comparison: Boxy vs. Oversized

| Feature | Boxy Fit | Oversized Fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Silhouette** | Square, structured drop-shoulder | Elongated, draped, slouchy |
| **Chest Ease** | +16–20 cm above body measurement | +24–32 cm above body measurement |
| **Length** | Hip-grazing to upper hip | Mid-hip to high-thigh |
| **Best For** | Layering, structured looks | Street style, relaxed draping |
| **Sizing Advice** | Size up if between sizes | True to size; size down for less drape |

## 8. Indian Body Type Reference

| Body Type | Recommended Fit |
|---|---|
| Lean / Athletic (170–178 cm, 58–72 kg) | Boxy M or Oversized S–M |
| Regular / Medium (170–178 cm, 73–85 kg) | Boxy L or Oversized M–L |
| Broad Shouldered (broader than 46 in chest) | Boxy XL or Oversized L |
| Tall (>180 cm) | Boxy L–XL (for longer body length) |
