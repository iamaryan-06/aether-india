# AETHER INDIA — Fabric Care Manual
**Document Version:** 1.4 | **Effective Date:** 1 June 2024

---

## 1. Introduction

AETHER INDIA products are crafted from premium, performance-grade fabrics. Improper care is the leading cause of colour fading, shrinkage, and structural breakdown. This manual provides care instructions by fabric type, GSM weight, and garment category.

> **Important:** Damage caused by incorrect washing, drying, or ironing is **not covered** under our return or exchange policy. Please follow these instructions carefully.

---

## 2. Understanding GSM (Grams per Square Metre)

GSM indicates the fabric weight per square metre. Higher GSM = heavier, denser, more durable fabric.

| GSM Range | Weight Class | Typical Garments | Care Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 120–180 GSM | Lightweight | Basic tees, liners | Easy care |
| 180–260 GSM | Mid-weight | Casual tees, polos | Moderate care |
| 260–380 GSM | Heavyweight | Sweatshirts, crews | Careful care |
| 380–520 GSM | Ultra-heavyweight | Boxy hoodies, jackets | Specialist care |

## 3. Care by Product Category

### 3.1 Heavyweight Hoodies & Sweatshirts (360–520 GSM)
*Applicable to: AETHER 480GSM Heavyweight Boxy Hoodie, AETHER Washed Crew Neck Sweatshirt*

**Washing:**
- Machine wash **cold** (max 30°C / 86°F) on a **gentle / delicate cycle**.
- Turn garment **inside-out** before washing to protect printed graphics and surface finish.
- Use a **mild, phosphate-free detergent** (e.g., Ariel Matic Liquid, Surf Excel Matic Liquid). Avoid powder detergents — they leave residue in thick fabrics.
- Wash with **similar colours only**. Do not wash with white garments.
- Use **no more than the recommended detergent dose** — excess detergent is hard to rinse out of 480 GSM fabric and causes stiffness.
- **Do not bleach.** Avoid any detergent containing optical brighteners.
- **Do not dry-clean** — solvents degrade the pigment-dye finish.

**Drying:**
- **Do not tumble dry.** High heat causes significant shrinkage (up to 8–12%) and weakens the cotton loops.
- Reshape the garment while damp and **lay flat to dry** in a shaded area.
- **Never hang dry a wet heavyweight hoodie** — the weight of water stretches the shoulders and distorts the shape.
- Drying time: 12–24 hours in an airy indoor space.

**Ironing:**
- Iron **inside-out** on **low heat** (cotton / ≤ 110°C setting).
- Place a **pressing cloth** between the iron and any graphic print areas.
- **Do not iron directly over the drawstring channel** — it crushes the cord.
- **Do not steam** if the hoodie has a stone-washed or enzyme-washed finish — steam re-activates shrinkage.

**Storage:**
- **Fold, do not hang.** Hanging heavy hoodies on thin hangers creates shoulder bumps.
- Store in a cool, dry, dark place. Avoid plastic bags; breathable cotton storage bags are ideal.
- Cedar blocks near storage prevent moth damage.

### 3.2 Graphic & Acid-Wash Tees (180–260 GSM)
*Applicable to: Vintage Acid-Wash Oversized Graphic Tee, AETHER Drop-Shoulder Oversized Tee*

**Washing:**
- Machine wash **cold** (max 30°C) or hand wash in cold water.
- Turn **inside-out** — especially critical for screen-printed and DTG graphics.
- Use a **colour-safe, mild liquid detergent**. Avoid fabric softeners on acid-wash pieces — they strip the intentional colour variation.
- Wash separately for the first **3 washes** as acid-wash tees may release excess dye.

**Drying:**
- **Tumble dry on low heat is acceptable** (unlike heavyweight hoodies), but **air drying is strongly preferred** to preserve print vibrancy.
- Remove from dryer while slightly damp and hang or lay flat to prevent wrinkles.
- **Do not over-dry** — high heat cracks screen-print inks.

**Ironing:**
- Iron **inside-out** on a **medium-low setting** (≤ 130°C).
- **Never iron directly over graphics or prints.**

### 3.3 Technical Outerwear & Shell Jackets
*Applicable to: Apex 3-Layer Gore-Tex Technical Shell Jacket*

**This is a technical performance garment. Incorrect care WILL compromise its waterproofing (DWR) and breathability.**

**Washing:**
- Machine wash **warm** (40°C / 104°F) — warm water is essential for reactivating DWR coating.
- Use a **technical outerwear detergent** specifically designed for DWR-coated fabrics (e.g., Nikwax Tech Wash). Standard detergents leave surfactant residue that clogs the Gore-Tex membrane and destroys breathability.
- **Do not use fabric softener, bleach, or stain removers** on the outer shell.
- Close all zips, velcro, and pockets before washing to prevent abrasion.
- Run an **extra rinse cycle** to flush all detergent thoroughly.

**Drying & DWR Reactivation:**
- **Tumble dry on low heat (40°C) for 20–30 minutes** — this step is **mandatory** to reactivate the Durable Water Repellency (DWR) treatment.
- Alternatively, hang dry then press with a **warm iron (no steam) through a pressing cloth** for 5–10 seconds per panel.
- If water no longer beads on the outer surface after washing, apply a **DWR re-treatment spray** (e.g., Nikwax TX.Direct Spray-On).

**Storage:**
- Hang on a **wide, padded hanger** — do not compress or fold for extended periods as it creases the Gore-Tex membrane.
- Store unzipped and in a breathable garment bag.

### 3.4 Denim & Woven Bottoms
*Applicable to: Distressed Denim Carpenter Jeans, Tactical Ripstop Parachute Cargo Pants*

**Denim Jeans:**
- Wash **inside-out in cold water** (max 30°C) to slow fading.
- Wash **infrequently** — after every **3–5 wears** is ideal. Spot-clean minor stains.
- **Do not tumble dry** — hang dry to prevent shrinkage.
- Iron inside-out on medium heat to reduce denim creasing.

**Ripstop Cargo Pants:**
- Machine wash cold on **regular cycle** — ripstop nylon/polyester blends are more durable than cotton.
- Tumble dry on **low heat** is acceptable for ripstop fabrics.
- Empty all pockets before washing — metal hardware in pockets can damage the water-resistant coating.

## 5. Stain Removal Guide

| Stain Type | Treatment | Safe For |
|---|---|---|
| Food / Oil | Blot (do not rub), apply dish soap directly, soak 10 min, cold wash | All fabrics |
| Ink | Dab with isopropyl alcohol on inside-out fabric, do not rub | All except acid-wash |
| Mud | Let dry completely, brush off, then wash cold | All fabrics |
| Sweat / Deodorant | Soak in cold water + white vinegar (1:3) for 1 hour | Cotton & denim |
| Blood | Cold water only (hot water sets blood). Hydrogen peroxide for whites | Cotton only |

> **Never use hot water on fresh stains** — heat bonds proteins (blood, food) and sets pigment dyes permanently.

## 6. Warranty & Care Defect Disclaimer

AETHER INDIA warrants its garments against manufacturing defects for **6 months from purchase**. This warranty does **not** cover:

- Colour fading due to incorrect washing temperature or frequency.
- Shrinkage caused by tumble drying heavyweight cotton garments.
- Graphic cracking caused by ironing directly over prints.
- DWR membrane failure caused by washing with standard detergents.
- Pilling caused by washing with rough fabrics (jeans, velcro items).
